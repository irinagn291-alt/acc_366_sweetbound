Guide a scarf-wearing frog across grassy banks, busy roads and rivers in this single-player arcade game.

Tap to hop forward, swipe in four directions or use the on-screen arrows. Dodge cars, navigate around rocks and ride moving logs. Your score is the furthest row reached, so revisiting a row won't add points.

Try a new random route in a classic crossing, or take on the daily expedition. Collect three amber signs on grassy banks to record wetland discoveries. You can retry the same day's route.

Visit the field notebook for illustrated instructions and an optional guided practice. Your journal keeps track of completed runs, discoveries and progress towards four badges.

The app interface is in English.
