# Sweetbound

## What it is

Sweetbound is an offline arcade game about guiding a frog across roads and rivers. It is for players who want to beat their distance record, try a daily route, or learn the crossing controls in practice.

## Launch and onboarding

1. A cold launch opens the reserve menu over the game field. It shows “WETLAND RESERVE”, the “Sweetbound” wordmark, “FIELD ARCADE”, “SERIES 01” and “PERSONAL BEST”, initially “0 rows”.
2. The main choices are “Start crossing”, “Daily expedition”, “How to play” and “Journal”. The initial daily caption is “One daily route · Three observations”.
3. There is no mandatory onboarding. “How to play” opens the field notebook, where “Start practice” begins a guided three-part crossing.

## Screens

### Reserve menu

- “Start crossing” starts a new crossing.
- “Daily expedition” starts the day’s route. Its caption changes to “Today's best — [distance] · Goal — 3 signs” after a result, or “All three observations recorded” after completion.
- “How to play” opens “Field notebook” on the “How to play” tab.
- “Journal” opens “Field notebook” on the “My journal” tab.
- The information icon, accessible as “About and privacy”, opens “About”.
- The speaker icon, accessible as “Mute sound” or “Unmute sound”, turns game sound off or on.

### Crossing

The field shows the frog, grass, roads, cars, rivers and moving logs. “DISTANCE” shows the number of new rows reached; daily play uses “DAILY EXPEDITION” instead.

- Tap the field to hop forward, or swipe up, down, left or right to hop in that direction.
- The arrow buttons are accessible as “Left”, “Back”, “Forward” and “Right” and move the frog accordingly.
- “Pause” opens the pause panel.

In “Daily expedition”, “OBSERVATIONS · [count] OF 3” tracks signs collected. The initial instruction is “Hop onto the amber sign on the grass.” After a find it says “Recorded — [observation]. Look for the next sign.” The observations are “Heron feather”, “Beaver tracks” and “Dragonfly over water”.

Practice shows “PRACTICE · [step] OF 3” and progresses through:

- “Your first hop”: “Tap the field or press ↑ to hop forward.”
- “Across the road”: “Wait for a gap between cars and cross the road in two hops.” Then: “One more hop forward takes you to safe grass.”
- “Ride the log”: “Wait for a log to line up with you, then hop on.” Then: “The log carries you along. Hop forward onto the bank!”

### Pause and results

The pause panel shows “TRAIL BREAK”, “Take a breather” and “Continue from the same spot when you're ready.” “Resume” continues the crossing; “Main menu” leaves it for the reserve. The sound control is also available.

A finished crossing shows “NEW RECORD” or “CROSSING COMPLETE”, a distance and one of these results:

- “Hit by a car!” with “Let the car pass, then hop across.”
- “Missed the log!” with “Wait for a log to line up with you.”
- “Swept away!” with “Hop off the log before it reaches the edge.”

“Cross again” starts another crossing; “Main menu” returns to the reserve.

A daily result shows “FIELD OBSERVATIONS”. An unfinished expedition reports “Observations: [count] of 3. The route stays the same for the rest of the day.” A completed one shows “Expedition complete!” and the three observation names followed by “All three observations added to your journal.” “Try expedition again” starts the daily route again; “Main menu” returns to the reserve.

Practice completion shows “PRACTICE COMPLETE”, “You made it across!” and “Now try a crossing on your own.” “Start crossing” begins a regular crossing; “Main menu” returns to the reserve.

### Field notebook

The “How to play” and “My journal” controls switch between the notebook’s two tabs. The close icon, accessible as “Close notebook”, returns to the reserve.

“How to play” contains:

- “How to score”: “Guide the frog as far as you can. Your first hop onto each new row earns one point. You can move back and choose another path.”
- “Start practice”, which closes the notebook and begins practice.
- “On the trail”, with “Hop”, “Road”, “River” and “Daily expedition” instructions explaining gestures, traffic, logs and collecting amber signs.

“My journal” shows “Personal best” and “Best distance in rows”; “Crossings” with “Completed runs”, “Total rows crossed”, “Today's expedition best, in rows” and “Successful expedition days”; and “Observations” with the three finds.

“Badges” shows progress toward:

- “First clearing” — “Reach row 4 in a single crossing.”
- “Across the river” — “Reach the bank beyond the first river at row 7.”
- “Field scout” — “Collect three signs in one daily expedition.”
- “Distant trails” — “Cross a total of 60 rows in completed runs.”

Completed badges show “Earned”; others show progress toward their goal.

### About

“About” shows “Sweetbound”, “Version 1.0” and: “An arcade game about a frog crossing busy roads and rivers. Play crossings and daily expeditions offline.”

“Privacy policy” opens “Privacy”. The bundled policy cannot be loaded in this build, so that screen says “The privacy policy could not be loaded.” “Close” dismisses the About sheet. There is no displayed online privacy or support link.

## Features

- Frog crossings with points for reaching new rows.
- “Daily expedition” with a shared route for the day and three observations.
- Guided “Start practice” with road and river lessons.
- “Personal best”, crossing totals, daily best, observations and “Badges” in “My journal”.
- Pause and resume, sound control, directional gestures and arrow buttons.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- Moving back or revisiting a row does not earn another point. Reach a new row to increase “DISTANCE”.
- A missed daily sign does not end the expedition. More signs appear ahead; collect three in one crossing.
- A failed daily attempt starts the same route again for the rest of the day. Its observation count starts over, while discoveries already added to the journal remain.
- In practice, a collision returns the frog to the current lesson’s safe bank. Follow the updated instruction and try again.
- Before a finished crossing, the journal says “Finish your first crossing to record a result.” Today’s expedition best is “None yet”, and undiscovered observations say “Not found yet”. Finish a crossing or collect signs to populate them.
- Leaving a crossing with “Main menu” abandons it. The app pauses a crossing when it becomes inactive; use “Resume” while the session remains open.

## Starter content and resume

The game includes a built-in field guide, practice route, observation names and badge goals. Journal statistics start empty, with a personal best of zero.

A paused crossing can be resumed from the same spot during the current session. An unfinished crossing is not restored after a fresh launch.

## Permissions

None.

## Absent

Login or accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, user-generated content, an account deletion flow and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are absent.

## Data and support

The personal best, journal progress and sound preference stay on the device. Crossings and daily expeditions can be played offline.

There is no on-screen “Contact support” control in this build.

## Scanning and health

None.

## Platform

The interface uses English regardless of the device’s language. The daily route follows the device’s local calendar day and time zone.

The app supports iPhone and iPad, with a minimum of iOS 17.0. Both support portrait and landscape; iPad also supports upside-down portrait.
